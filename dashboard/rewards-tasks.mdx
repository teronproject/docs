The Teron dashboard includes two related sections — Rewards and Tasks — that let you track what you have earned on the platform and find ways to earn more. The Rewards section shows your current TERR balance and a history of how you accumulated it. The Tasks section lists community actions you can complete to earn additional TERR.

<Note>
  TERR is a platform reward token tracked within the Teron platform. It is used for platform credits and rewards. Check the [TERR token guide](/rewards/terr-token) for details on what TERR represents and how it works.
</Note>

## Rewards section

The Rewards tab in your dashboard gives you a clear view of your TERR earnings:

- **Current TERR balance** — the total TERR credited to your account right now
- **Reward history** — a chronological list of every TERR credit, showing the amount earned and what triggered it
- **Earning source** — each history entry indicates whether TERR came from completing a task, a referral, or another platform activity

To view your rewards, navigate to [teron.io/dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard) with your wallet connected and select the **Rewards** tab.

## Tasks section

The Tasks section, also accessible at [teron.io/dashboard/tasks](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks), shows you what community actions are currently available and what you have already done.

From the Tasks section you can see:

- **Available tasks** — actions you can take right now to earn TERR, such as following Teron on social platforms, joining the Discord, or launching a token on testnet
- **Completed tasks** — a record of tasks you have already finished
- **TERR per task** — how much TERR each task awards before you commit to completing it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tasks section">
    Go to [teron.io/dashboard/tasks](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks) or click the **Tasks** tab inside your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse available tasks">
    Review the list of tasks and the TERR amount each one offers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete a task">
    Follow the instructions for each task. Most tasks involve an on-platform or social action that Teron can verify automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your Rewards tab">
    After a task is verified, the TERR credit will appear in your Rewards history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The list of available tasks changes over time as Teron adds new community actions. It is worth checking back regularly to see what is currently on offer.
</Info>

<Warning>
  The rewards and tasks features can be paused by Teron for maintenance or other operational reasons. If either feature is currently unavailable, you will see a maintenance notice in the relevant section. No TERR is lost during a pause — your existing balance and history remain intact.
</Warning>
