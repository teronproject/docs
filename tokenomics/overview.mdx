Teron uses two connected tokens on BNB Chain. Each one has a distinct role, and together they form the foundation of the Teron economy.

## The Two Tokens

| Token | Full Name | Ticker | Total Supply | Network | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **TER** | Teron | TER | 1,000,000,000 | BNB Chain | Ecosystem utility token |
| **TERR** | Teron Reward | TERR | 10,000,000,000 | BNB Chain | Platform reward token |

- **TER** supports platform utility, ecosystem growth, creator incentives, and future Teron products.
- **TERR** rewards participation and eligible activity across the Teron platform.

TER and TERR are separate tokens with different purposes. Holding one does not automatically give you the other.

## Tokenomics Philosophy

Teron's token model is designed around a simple relationship:

> Users participate → users earn TERR → Teron grows → TER supports the wider ecosystem.

The goal is to connect token utility to actual platform activity, rather than creating token utility that exists only for marketing.

## Token Supply Principles

Teron aims to keep supply information transparent. Across TER and TERR, the documentation covers:

- Total supply
- Allocation
- Vesting
- Unlock schedules
- Reward reserves
- Liquidity allocation
- Treasury allocation
- Ecosystem allocation
- Future supply changes, if any

Any material changes to these areas will be reflected in the documentation and communicated through official Teron channels.

## What Teron Does Not Promise

Teron does not make forward-looking claims about token price or performance. In particular, the documentation and official communications will not:

- Claim that TER or TERR will increase in price
- Promise profits
- Promise an exchange listing
- Describe either token as guaranteed to have future value
- Use language such as "100x", "guaranteed returns", "get rich", or "buy before it's too late"

If you see such claims attributed to Teron outside of official channels, treat them as unofficial.

## Live, Planned, and Future Functionality

Throughout the tokenomics documentation, features are grouped by status:

- **Live** — Available today on the Teron platform.
- **Planned** — Announced by Teron with defined intent but not yet active.
- **Future** — Longer-term direction that may change before it is finalized.

Any page describing utility, vesting, conversion, or launch details will label each item accordingly.
