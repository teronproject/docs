TER is the main ecosystem token of Teron. It is intended to support platform utility, ecosystem growth, creator incentives, and future Teron products.

## Token Details

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Teron |
| **Ticker** | TER |
| **Total Supply** | 1,000,000,000 TER |
| **Network** | BNB Chain |

## TER Utility

TER is designed to support a range of features across the Teron platform. All utility on this page is **planned** unless explicitly marked as live. Teron will update this section as each utility becomes active.

<Info>
  The utilities listed below describe the intended role of TER. They are **planned** functionality unless a specific item is marked as live. This page will be updated as features are released.
</Info>

Planned uses for TER include:

- Premium Teron features
- Advanced launch tools
- Reduced platform fees
- Premium project pages
- Advanced analytics
- Creator tools
- Builder incentives
- Ecosystem programs
- Future Teron products

As each utility becomes active, it will move from the planned list into a live section on this page.

## Team Vesting (Planned)

The current planned team allocation is **100,000,000 TER** (10% of total supply).

The planned vesting structure is:

- **Cliff:** 12 months
- **Linear vesting:** 24–36 months after the cliff

<Note>
  This vesting structure is **planned**. It is not final until Teron officially publishes the final team vesting schedule. Values here may change before launch.
</Note>

## Initial Circulating Supply

Teron is targeting a controlled initial circulating supply for TER. The final initial circulating supply has not been decided yet and will be published before the TER launch.

A few things to keep in mind:

- The full 1,000,000,000 TER total supply will **not** enter circulation at launch.
- Allocation categories such as Team, Treasury, Partnerships, Advisors, and portions of Ecosystem, Rewards, and Marketing are subject to vesting or scheduled release, so they are not part of initial circulating supply.
- Final circulating supply figures, unlock schedules, and any launch-time details will be communicated through official Teron channels before launch.

<Warning>
  Any circulating supply figure attributed to Teron before the official launch announcement is unofficial. Do not rely on numbers from third parties.
</Warning>
