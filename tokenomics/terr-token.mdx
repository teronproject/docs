TERR is Teron's platform reward token. It is designed to reward participation and eligible activity across the Teron platform on BNB Chain.

## Token Details

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Teron Reward |
| **Ticker** | TERR |
| **Total Supply** | 10,000,000,000 TERR |
| **Network** | BNB Chain |

## How to Earn TERR

Users may earn TERR through:

- Successful token creation
- Teron tasks
- Community campaigns
- Referrals
- Eligible builder activities
- Other platform activities announced by Teron

Specific reward amounts and eligibility rules are set by Teron and may change over time. See [Earning TERR](/rewards/earning-terr) and [Tasks](/rewards/tasks) for the current earning flows.

## How TERR Is Positioned

TERR is a **reward token**. It reflects your activity and participation on the Teron platform.

<Note>
  TERR is not an investment product or a promise of future value. Do not treat TERR as a financial asset or assume it carries a fixed monetary value.
</Note>

Specifically:

- TERR is not sold directly by Teron.
- TERR is not a guarantee of financial return.
- Earning TERR does not imply any investment opportunity.
- Teron does not promise a future price, listing, or exchange for TERR.

## TERR vs. TER

TERR and TER are separate tokens with different purposes:

| Token | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **TERR** | Rewards and participation |
| **TER** | Teron ecosystem utility |

Eligible TERR may become convertible to TER through a future Teron conversion mechanism. See [TER and TERR Conversion](/tokenomics/conversion) for the current status of that feature.
