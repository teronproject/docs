Every token deployed through Teron gets a public project page — a shareable profile that shows your token's details, social links, and project information to anyone on the internet. It is the front door to your project for potential community members and researchers looking at your BNB Chain token.

## What is a project page?

A project page is a public token profile hosted by Teron at a fixed URL based on your token's name or symbol:

```
https://teron.io/t/{token_name}
```

For example, if your token symbol is `MYTKN`, your project page would be at `https://teron.io/t/MYTKN`. You can share this link in announcements, social posts, or anywhere you want people to learn more about your BEP-20 project.

<Info>
  Project pages are publicly accessible. Anyone can view your token profile without creating an account or connecting a wallet.
</Info>

## What appears on a project page?

Depending on what information you have added through your dashboard, a project page can display:

- **Token name and symbol** — set during deployment and shown automatically
- **Logo** — a visual identity for your token
- **Description** — a plain-text summary of what your project is about
- **Contract address** — the on-chain address of your deployed BEP-20 contract
- **Supply and decimals** — the total token supply and decimal configuration
- **Website URL** — a link to your project's main site
- **Social links** — X (Twitter), Telegram, Discord, GitHub, and Whitepaper
- **Tokenomics** — token allocation, distribution details, and related information
- **Roadmap** — planned milestones and development goals
- **Other project information** — any additional details you choose to publish

Not all fields are required. You can publish a page with only the basics and add more detail over time.

## The Teron leaderboard

All projects created through Teron appear in the project directory at [teron.io/leaderboard](https://teron.io/leaderboard). This gives your token passive visibility to people browsing the platform looking for projects on BNB Chain. A complete, well-filled profile stands out in the directory.

<Tip>
  A project page that includes a logo, a clear description, and at least one or two social links gives visitors enough context to understand your project. Incomplete pages with no description or links are harder for people to evaluate.
</Tip>

## Managing your project page

You control everything on your project page through the Teron dashboard. Publishing a project page is optional — your token contract exists on-chain regardless — but it is the recommended way to give your project visibility and make it easy for your community to find information in one place.
