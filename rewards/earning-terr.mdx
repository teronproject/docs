Teron rewards active participants with TERR (Teron Reward), the platform reward token. There are several ways to earn TERR, ranging from using the platform to engaging with the community. All TERR you earn accumulates in your rewards balance and is visible from your dashboard.

## Ways to Earn TERR

### 1. Eligible Platform Activities

Certain actions you take directly on Teron can earn you TERR. The most notable example is creating and deploying a token on BNB Chain. As Teron grows, additional platform activities may become eligible for token rewards.

### 2. Completing Community Tasks

Teron publishes a set of community tasks you can complete to earn TERR. These include actions like following Teron on X, liking or reposting official posts, and joining official Teron communities. Tasks are managed through the [tasks dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks).

### 3. Participating in Campaigns

From time to time, Teron runs campaigns that offer TERR rewards for specific participation. Campaign availability varies — keep an eye on official Teron channels to stay informed when new campaigns go live.

<Note>
  Specific reward amounts for each activity are determined by Teron and can change at any time. This documentation does not quote fixed reward figures — always check the dashboard for current values.
</Note>

## Tracking Your Rewards

All TERR you earn is credited to your account automatically. You can see your current balance and full reward history from the [Teron dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard).

<Info>
  Your TERR rewards accumulate in the **Rewards** section of the dashboard. You can review your full reward history there at any time.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Check the [tasks dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks) regularly — new earning opportunities are added over time as Teron introduces new tasks and campaigns.
</Tip>

## Availability

The rewards and tasks features are managed by Teron's admin system. This means the set of available activities and tasks can change, and new earning opportunities may be introduced without prior notice.

<Warning>
  Teron may temporarily pause the rewards and tasks features for maintenance or platform updates. During a pause, tasks cannot be completed and TERR cannot be earned until the feature is restored. Check the dashboard or official Teron channels for status updates.
</Warning>
