Teron's community task system gives you a straightforward way to earn TERR (Teron Reward) by engaging with the Teron ecosystem. Tasks are published by Teron and cover actions like following official accounts, interacting with posts, and joining communities. Once you complete a task and it is verified, TERR is credited to your account.

You can find all available tasks at the [tasks dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks).

## Types of Tasks

Teron currently publishes the following types of community tasks:

- **Follow on X** — Follow the official Teron account ([@teronapp](https://x.com/teronapp)) on X
- **Like a post** — Like an official Teron post on X
- **Repost a post** — Repost an official Teron post on X
- **Join a community** — Join an official Teron community channel
- **Participate in a campaign** — Take part in a time-limited campaign run by Teron

<Note>
  The tasks listed above are examples of the types currently available. Available tasks are controlled by Teron's admin system and change over time — new tasks can be added and existing ones can be removed or updated at any point.
</Note>

## How to Complete a Task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the tasks dashboard">
    Open the [tasks dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks). You will see a list of all currently available tasks and the TERR reward associated with each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a task">
    Choose a task you want to complete. Read the task description carefully so you understand exactly what action is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the required action">
    Carry out the action off-platform — for example, follow [@teronapp](https://x.com/teronapp) on X, like a specified post, or join a linked community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit or verify your completion">
    Return to the tasks dashboard and submit your completion. Some tasks include a verification step where Teron confirms the action was taken before crediting your reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn TERR">
    Once your task completion is verified, TERR is credited to your rewards balance. You can check your balance and history from the [main dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verification and Timing

Not all tasks are credited instantly. Some require Teron to verify that the required action was genuinely completed before TERR is awarded.

<Info>
  Task completions are verified before TERR is credited to your account. Depending on the task type, verification may take some time. If your reward does not appear immediately, allow some time before following up.
</Info>

## Fair Participation

Tasks are designed to reward genuine engagement with the Teron community. Attempting to falsify or game task completion undermines the system and is not permitted.

<Warning>
  Do not attempt to fake or manipulate task completions — for example, by using bot accounts, creating multiple accounts, or submitting false verification. Teron may forfeit rewards and restrict access for accounts found to be abusing the task system.
</Warning>

## Task Availability

The tasks feature is managed by Teron's admin system. Teron may add, remove, or update tasks at any time, and the feature itself can be paused during platform maintenance. Visit the [tasks dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks) for the most up-to-date list of available tasks.
