Teron operates with two separate tokens: TER and TERR. They have different roles, different ways of being obtained, and separate purposes within the platform. Understanding the distinction between them is important before engaging with Teron's Web3 rewards system on BNB Chain.

## The Two Tokens at a Glance

| Token | Symbol | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Teron** | TER | The main Teron ecosystem token |
| **Teron Reward** | TERR | The platform reward token earned through activities and tasks |

These are entirely separate tokens. Having one does not give you the other, and they are not currently interchangeable.

## TER — The Ecosystem Token

TER is Teron's primary ecosystem token. It represents the main token for the Teron platform on BNB Chain. TER is not earned through the platform's reward system — it exists separately from the tasks and rewards features.

## TERR — The Platform Reward Token

TERR (Teron Reward) is what you earn by participating in Teron's platform activities and community tasks. This includes actions like deploying a token, completing social tasks, and joining campaigns. TERR accumulates in your rewards balance on the dashboard as you engage with the platform.

TERR is not available for purchase through Teron. It is earned through participation.

<Info>
  TERR is currently earned through platform use and community tasks. Its broader utility — including any future integrations within the Teron ecosystem — is planned for future releases. Teron will share updates through official channels as new functionality becomes available.
</Info>

## The Relationship Between TER and TERR

TER and TERR serve different purposes today, but Teron has plans to connect them more closely in the future. Specifically, the ability to convert TERR into TER is a **planned, coming-soon feature**.

This feature has not launched yet. No conversion mechanism is currently active.

<Note>
  No official TERR-to-TER conversion rate has been announced. Do not rely on any unofficial figures you may see elsewhere — they are not endorsed by Teron. When conversion becomes available, the details will be communicated through official Teron channels only.
</Note>

## What TERR Is Not

To be transparent about what you are earning:

- TERR is not a guarantee of financial return
- TERR does not have a fixed monetary value
- TERR is not a substitute for TER
- Earning TERR does not imply any investment opportunity

TERR reflects your activity and participation on the Teron platform. Its utility may expand over time as the platform develops.

## Frequently Asked Questions

  No. TERR is earned through eligible platform activities and community tasks. Teron does not sell TERR directly.
</Accordion>

  Not yet. Conversion from TERR to TER is a planned feature that has not launched. Teron will announce when it becomes available.
</Accordion>
