Teron has two separate tokens that serve distinct purposes within the platform. Understanding the difference between them helps you make sense of how the Web3 rewards system works and what you can do with what you earn.

## What is TERR?

TERR (Teron Reward) is the platform reward token. You earn TERR by taking part in eligible Teron activities — things like deploying a token, completing community tasks, or participating in campaigns. It is not a token you purchase through Teron; it accumulates in your rewards balance as you engage with the platform.

You can view your current TERR balance and reward history from the [Teron dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard).

## TERR vs. TER

These two tokens are separate and serve different roles:

| Token | Full Name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **TER** | Teron | The main Teron ecosystem token |
| **TERR** | Teron Reward | The platform reward token earned through activities and tasks |

TER is the primary ecosystem token for Teron on BNB Chain. TERR is the token you receive as a reward for being an active participant on the platform. They are not interchangeable at this time.

## TERR Utility

Right now, TERR functions as a platform reward token — a record of your participation and contribution to the Teron ecosystem. Teron plans to expand the ways TERR can be used over time as the platform grows.

<Note>
  TERR is a platform reward token. Its use cases and utility may expand over time. Do not treat TERR as a financial asset or assume it carries a fixed monetary value.
</Note>

<Info>
  A feature to convert TERR to TER is **planned** but has not yet launched. No conversion rate has been officially announced. When this feature becomes available, Teron will communicate the details through official channels.
</Info>

## How TERR Accumulates

When you complete an eligible action — such as creating a token on BNB Chain, finishing a community task, or joining a campaign — the corresponding TERR reward is credited to your account. You do not need to claim it manually; it appears in the Rewards section of your dashboard.

Specific reward amounts for each activity are set by Teron and may change over time. Check the [tasks dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard/tasks) to see what is currently available.
