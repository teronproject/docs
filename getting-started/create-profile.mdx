Teron does not use traditional accounts. The moment you connect your BNB Chain wallet, your profile is created and ready to use. There is no registration form, no email verification, and no password to set up or remember.

## How Your Profile Is Created

Your wallet address is your identity on Teron. When you connect your wallet for the first time, Teron reads your address and provisions a profile linked to it automatically. You are taken directly to your [dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard) without any additional steps.

Because your profile is tied to your wallet address, it persists across devices and browsers. Connecting the same wallet from a different computer gives you full access to the same profile and all your projects.

## What Your Profile Includes

Your Teron profile brings together everything related to your activity on the platform:

- **Projects** — all the tokens you have deployed through Teron
- **Deployments** — a record of each contract deployment and its status
- **Rewards** — any BNB or platform credits earned through referrals or tasks
- **Tasks** — available and completed tasks from the Task to Earn program
- **Public pages** — each of your tokens gets its own public-facing profile page on the Teron leaderboard

## Optional Profile Information

You can add a display name and other profile details from your [dashboard](https://teron.io/dashboard) at any time. This is optional — your wallet address is enough to use all core features.

<Info>
  All on-chain activity — including contract deployments and transactions — is recorded permanently on BNB Chain and is publicly visible on block explorers like BscScan. This is true regardless of what information you do or do not add to your Teron profile. Blockchain transactions are public by design.
</Info>

## Switching Wallets

If you connect a different wallet address, Teron will treat it as a separate profile. Projects and activity are always associated with the wallet address used to create them, so make sure you connect the same wallet each time if you want to access an existing profile.
